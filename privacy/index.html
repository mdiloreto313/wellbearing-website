Well-Bearing

Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

Well-Bearing is designed to help you connect your everyday actions with the life you want to live. Because that can involve personal information, privacy and user control are important parts of how Well-Bearing is designed.

1. Information you choose to provide

Well-Bearing may store information that you enter into the app in order to provide its features. Depending on how you use Well-Bearing, this may include information such as:

  • Life domains, goals and projects
  • Identity statements
  • Momentum items and completion history
  • Bearing Checks, reflections and related entries
  • Health measurements or other information you manually enter
  • App preferences and settings

You choose which Well-Bearing features to use and what information to enter into them.

2. Apple Health

Health access is optional. Well-Bearing may request permission to read or write specific types of health and fitness information through Apple Health when those features are enabled.

Depending on the features you choose to use and the permissions you grant, this may include information such as workouts, steps, sleep, body weight and blood pressure.

Apple Health permissions are controlled by you through iOS. Well-Bearing can only access the categories of Apple Health information for which you grant permission.

Well-Bearing uses health and fitness information to provide user-requested app functionality, such as displaying relevant information, recording permitted measurements, and supporting completion or progress features.

Well-Bearing does not use Apple Health data for advertising or sell Apple Health data.

3. How information is used

Information available to Well-Bearing may be used to operate and improve the features you choose to use, including to:

  • Display your goals, priorities, progress and history
  • Determine the information presented within the app
  • Support reminders, widgets and other requested app functionality
  • Generate summaries and reports requested by you
  • Maintain your preferences and app configuration
  • Diagnose technical problems and maintain app reliability

4. Advertising and sale of personal information

Well-Bearing does not sell your personal information.

Well-Bearing does not use your personal information or health information for third-party advertising.

5. Data sharing

Well-Bearing does not share your personal or health information with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by Apple platform services when necessary to provide functionality you choose to use, such as Apple Health, notifications, widgets, device storage, backup or other operating-system services, subject to Apple's applicable settings and policies.

6. Storage and security

Well-Bearing is designed to use the security and privacy protections provided by iOS and Apple platform technologies. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information handled by the app.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and control

Well-Bearing is designed around user choice. You may choose which features to use, what information to enter, and whether to grant optional permissions.

Apple Health permissions can be reviewed or changed through the appropriate iOS privacy and Health settings.

8. Children

Well-Bearing is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Well-Bearing evolves. When material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted here and the effective date will be revised.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy in Well-Bearing, contact:
