Well-Bearing

Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Well-Bearing is designed to help you connect your everyday actions with the life you want to live. Because that can involve personal information, privacy and user control are important parts of how Well-Bearing is designed. This Privacy Policy describes how Well-Bearing, LLC handles information in the Well-Bearing apps for Apple and Android and related website resources. It describes current release behavior.

1. Information you choose to provide

Well-Bearing may store information that you enter into the app in order to provide its features. Depending on how you use Well-Bearing, this may include information such as:

  • Life Domains, Goals, Projects, and Tasks
  • Identity Statements
  • Momentum Items and completion history
  • Behavior Change data
  • Bearing Checks, reflections, and related entries
  • Medication information you choose to enter
  • Health measurements or other information you manually enter
  • App preferences and settings
  • Generated-report source data used to create summaries you request

You choose which Well-Bearing features to use and what information to enter into them.

2. Local-first design

Core Well-Bearing personal information is generally stored locally on your device. That local-first design includes the categories listed above when you use those features.

Production cloud backup is not enabled in this release. Production multi-device sync is not enabled. Well-Bearing does not automatically upload your primary on-device wellness journal, medications, or report content to a server merely because you use the app.

Platform services may still process information when needed to provide functionality you choose to use — for example operating-system notifications, device storage or the device’s own backup, Apple Health / Health Connect, Screen Time-related services, Usage Access, App Store or Google Play subscription services, and similar platform infrastructure. Optional account or referral features may also use Well-Bearing-controlled network services when you choose to use them. This Privacy Policy describes current release behavior and does not claim that no external platform processing ever occurs.

3. Optional account

You may use Well-Bearing without creating an account (Continue without an account on the welcome screen). An optional account can unlock account-based features, such as referral features when those features are available. Sign-in is not required for ordinary local use.

If you sign in, Well-Bearing may process account identifiers and authentication information for that optional account. Your core wellness information is not synced to the cloud in this release. Creating an account does not turn on cloud backup or multi-device sync.

4. Apple Health and Health Connect

Health access is optional. On Apple devices, Well-Bearing may request permission to read or write specific types of health and fitness information through Apple Health when those features are enabled.

On Android, Health Connect access is also optional. When you explicitly authorize it, Well-Bearing may access supported health and wellness information through Health Connect.

Depending on the features you choose and the permissions you grant, supported reads may include workouts, sleep, steps, body weight, blood pressure, and related activity information. Well-Bearing can only access categories you authorize. It does not receive all Apple Health or Health Connect information.

When you use a supported write-back feature, Well-Bearing may write certain values that you initiate in the app back to Apple Health or Health Connect, according to platform permissions:

  • On iOS (Apple Health), supported write-back currently includes weight, blood pressure, and mindfulness sessions.
  • On Android (Health Connect), supported write-back currently includes weight and blood pressure.

Well-Bearing does not delete external Apple Health or Health Connect samples. Those external samples are not copied into a Recovery file as app-owned data. Health-derived history that Well-Bearing stores locally after you connect may remain on the device. Well-Bearing does not infer medication decisions from health data.

You can review or change Apple Health permissions in iOS Settings, and Health Connect permissions in Android settings. Well-Bearing does not use Apple Health or Health Connect data for advertising or tracking, and does not sell that data.

5. Encrypted Recovery files

Recovery is a file you create and keep. It is not automatic cloud backup, and it is not cloud synchronization. Production cloud backup is not enabled in this release.

When you choose Backup & Restore, you can create an encrypted Recovery file. Well-Bearing creates that file only when you ask for it.

  • The file uses the .wbre name and is encrypted with AES-GCM.
  • You choose a passphrase of at least 12 characters. Well-Bearing does not store that passphrase and cannot recover it if you forget it.
  • You save the file wherever you choose, such as Files, a computer, or another place you control. Well-Bearing does not upload or retain the file for you.
  • You can use the file after reinstalling Well-Bearing, on this device or another supported device, if you still have the file and the passphrase.
  • Restoring a Recovery file replaces Well-Bearing data in the current profile. It does not merge with the data already there.
  • Anyone who has both the file and the passphrase may be able to restore its contents.

The file may include app-owned Well-Bearing records, including health entries you record in Well-Bearing, such as weight, blood pressure, and mindfulness sessions stored by the app.

The Recovery file does not include, and restoring it does not restore:

  • External Apple Health or Health Connect samples
  • Accountability relationships, invitations, or sharing
  • Referral attribution
  • Device permissions, or subscription and account credentials
  • Digital Well-Being or Screen Time selections
  • Privacy Lock settings
  • Operating-system widget setup

6. Reports and Complete Data Copy

Reports such as a Therapy Planning Summary or Health Provider Summary are generated locally on your device. You choose whether and where to share a PDF. Generating a report does not by itself upload report content to a Well-Bearing server.

Export Complete Data Copy (from Me → Reports, and from Settings where shown) creates a JSON file and presents it through the system share sheet. The filename looks like WellBearingExport-<timestamp>.json. That file can contain highly sensitive content, including notes, Identity text, and medication records. It is a copy of Well-Bearing data on the device. It is not a guaranteed backup, and there is no separate production import for that JSON export. Raw Apple Health and Health Connect samples are not included.

Only share export files and Recovery files with people or services you trust. A destination you choose in the share sheet is outside Well-Bearing’s control.

7. Screen Time and Digital Well-Being

Apple: Where available, Well-Bearing may use Apple Screen Time / Family Controls-related functionality to support Digital Well-Being Momentum Items you configure. Those capabilities are provided through Apple platform services and remain subject to Apple’s settings and permissions.

Android: Android users may optionally grant Usage Access for supported Digital Well-Being features. When granted through Android system settings, Well-Bearing may use device/app usage information to:

  • Measure supported screen-time / app-usage behavior
  • Evaluate configured Digital Well-Being Momentum Items
  • Provide related completion or status information

Usage Access is optional and may be revoked. Usage information is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Android capabilities differ from Apple Screen Time capabilities. Well-Bearing does not read Google’s private Digital Wellbeing dashboard database.

These features are for individual self-monitoring on your device. They are not parental controls, child monitoring, or employer surveillance, and the current product does not block or shield other apps. Screen Time or Digital Well-Being selections are not restored by a Recovery file.

8. Local notifications

If you enable reminders, Well-Bearing may schedule local notifications through the operating system for Tasks, Momentum Items, and medications. Notification permission is optional where the platform requires it. Medication reminders are designed not to show the medication name or dose on the lock screen.

9. Medication information

Medication information you enter is treated as sensitive personal information and is stored locally. Medication reminders you enable are local notifications. They are designed not to expose drug names or doses on the lock screen.

Medication records help you record outcomes such as Taken, Skipped, or Not Recorded. Well-Bearing does not provide diagnosis, treatment advice, dosage recommendations, emergency support, or clinician monitoring. Therapy Planning summaries do not include medication details. A Health Provider summary can include medications you choose to include. Accountability Partner sharing is not available in this release, so medication details are not shared that way.

10. Subscriptions and billing

Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Well-Bearing may receive product, purchase, or entitlement information needed to determine trial and subscription access and to restore paid access. Well-Bearing does not receive your complete payment-card details from marketplace purchases and does not itself process marketplace credit-card transactions.

11. How information is used

Information available to Well-Bearing may be used to operate and improve the features you choose to use, including to:

  • Display your goals, priorities, progress and history
  • Determine the information presented within the app
  • Support reminders, widgets and other requested app functionality
  • Generate summaries and reports requested by you
  • Maintain your preferences and app configuration
  • Determine trial or subscription access
  • Operate optional account or referral features you choose to use

12. Advertising, tracking, and sale of personal information

Well-Bearing does not sell your personal information.

The current release does not include a third-party advertising SDK, a third-party analytics SDK, a session-replay SDK, or a third-party crash-reporting SDK. Well-Bearing does not use your personal information or health information for third-party advertising, and does not engage in cross-app tracking for advertising purposes. On-device product instrumentation, if present, does not send your wellness content to a third-party analytics provider.

13. Data sharing, referral, and Accountability Partners

Well-Bearing does not share your personal or health information with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by Apple or Google platform services when necessary to provide functionality you choose to use, such as Apple Health, Health Connect, notifications, widgets, device storage, the device’s own backup, Screen Time-related services, Usage Access, App Store or Google Play subscription services, or other operating-system services, subject to those platforms’ settings and policies.

If referral features are available and you choose to use them, Well-Bearing may process the referral information needed to apply an invitation. iOS App Store and TestFlight builds can offer referral. Android production referral is not enabled in this release. Referral attribution is not included in a Recovery file.

Accountability Partner networking is not enabled in this release. The production screen says Coming Soon. Well-Bearing does not currently share your information with Accountability Partners.

14. Storage and security

Well-Bearing is designed to use the security and privacy protections provided by iOS, Android, and related platform technologies. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information handled by the app.

Optional Privacy Lock can require Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode before showing app content, where the platform supports it. Well-Bearing does not store a custom app password for Privacy Lock. Privacy Lock settings are not restored by a Recovery file.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. A Recovery file is encrypted with the passphrase you choose. That is not the same as automatic cloud backup or server-side sync.

15. Your choices and control

Well-Bearing is designed around user choice. You may choose which features to use, what information to enter, and whether to grant optional permissions.

Apple Health permissions can be reviewed or changed through the appropriate iOS privacy and Health settings. Health Connect and Usage Access permissions can be reviewed or changed through the appropriate Android settings. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through your App Store or Google Play account settings.

16. Account deletion

You can delete your Well-Bearing account in the app via Me → Account → Delete Account.

If you cannot access the app, you may request deletion through the account deletion page or by emailing wellbearingapp@gmail.com. Identity verification may be required.

When deletion completes, Well-Bearing deletes your Well-Bearing account and Well-Bearing-controlled account and cloud data associated with your account (when present). Well-Bearing does not retain account profile data after deletion completes.

Apple Health data, Health Connect data, and App Store / Google Play purchase history and subscription records are outside Well-Bearing’s deletion authority and are not deleted by Well-Bearing. Deleting your account does not cancel App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

17. Children

Well-Bearing is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

18. What this policy does not claim

Well-Bearing does not claim:

  • HIPAA certification or medical-device status
  • That a Recovery file is automatic cloud backup or multi-device sync
  • That Well-Bearing can recover a forgotten Recovery passphrase
  • That production cloud backup or production multi-device sync is enabled in this release
  • That Accountability Partner sharing is currently available
  • That deleting a Well-Bearing account deletes Apple Health data, Health Connect data, or App Store / Google Play billing records
  • That deleting a Well-Bearing account cancels an App Store or Google Play subscription

19. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Well-Bearing evolves. When material changes are made, Well-Bearing, LLC will post the updated policy here and revise the effective date.
