Delete your Well-Bearing account

You can delete your Well-Bearing account at any time. This page explains how to request deletion and what data is and is not removed.

Easiest method: delete in the app

The simplest way to delete your account is inside Well-Bearing:

Open Well-Bearing → Me → Account → Delete Account

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot open Well-Bearing or complete deletion in the app, you may request account deletion by email.

Email: wellbearingapp@gmail.com

Use the subject line Well-Bearing Account Deletion Request (the link above pre-fills that subject).

Identity verification may be required before an account can be deleted, so that only the account owner can request removal.

What is deleted

When deletion completes, Well-Bearing deletes:

  • Your Well-Bearing account
  • Well-Bearing-controlled account and cloud data associated with your account (when present)

Well-Bearing does not retain account profile data after deletion completes.

What is not deleted by Well-Bearing

The following are outside Well-Bearing’s deletion authority and are not deleted by Well-Bearing when you delete your account:

  • Apple Health data
  • Health Connect data
  • App Store / Google Play purchase history and subscription records

Subscriptions

Deleting your Well-Bearing account does not cancel App Store or Google Play subscriptions. To stop billing, manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
