Well-Bearing Support

Need help with Well-Bearing? We’re here to help you get back on course.

Questions or technical issues

If something isn’t working as expected, or if you have a question about using Well-Bearing, please contact us.

Email: support@wellbearing.app

When reporting a technical issue, it helps to include:

  • Your iPhone model
  • Your iOS version
  • A brief description of what happened
  • The steps you took before the issue occurred
  • A screenshot, when helpful

Feedback

Well-Bearing is designed to be personal, flexible and useful in everyday life. If something feels confusing, unnecessary, difficult to use or particularly valuable, we’d like to hear about it.

Your data

Well-Bearing is built around user choice and control. Questions about privacy, permissions or your data can also be sent to support@wellbearing.app.
